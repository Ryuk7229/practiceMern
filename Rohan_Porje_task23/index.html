ABOUT GREENWOOD

A library built around people and ideas.

Greenwood is more than a place to borrow books. It is a welcoming space where people can read, study, work and spend time together. Our library brings together a wide collection of books and resources for readers of all ages.

For more than 40 years, Greenwood has been part of the local community. From quiet reading areas to comfortable spaces for learning, we try to make everyone feel at home.

We also host events, workshops and community activities throughout the year. These events give people a chance to learn something new, share ideas and connect with others.

Along with our physical collection, members can access digital resources and enjoy free Wi-Fi. Whether you are looking for your next great book or simply need a peaceful place to work, Greenwood is open to everyone.
